# 📄 A Generative AI-Driven Predictive Analytics Framework (2026)

## 📌 Summary
Introduces a GenAI-driven framework using LSTM and Transformer-based multimodal fusion to optimize engineering design workflows and measure innovation via a "Creativity Index (CI)".

## 📊 Performance Gains
- **Engineering Design:** +15% improvement in performance.
- **Communication Effectiveness:** +16% increase.
- **Teamwork/Collaboration:** +16% increase.
- **Predictive Accuracy:** 89% accuracy (RMSE 3.8) in predicting design outcomes.

## 💡 Core Innovations
- **Creativity Index (CI):** Quantifies innovation by combining novelty and feasibility metrics.
- **Adaptive Learning:** Real-time feedback loops allowing early risk detection in design iterations.
