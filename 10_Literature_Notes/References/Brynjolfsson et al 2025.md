# 📄 Generative AI at Work (2025)

## 📌 Summary
A landmark empirical study of 5,179 customer support agents showing that generative AI acts as a "skill-leveler", disproportionately benefiting novice workers.

## 📊 Key Findings
- **Average Productivity:** +14-15% increase in issues resolved per hour.
- **Novice Workers:** **+34-36% increase** in productivity.
- **Expert Workers:** Minimal to no impact (~0%).
- **Quality:** Customer sentiment improved; manager escalations fell by 25%.

## 🚀 Mechanisms
- **Dissemination of Tacit Knowledge:** AI captures patterns from top performers and provides them as suggestions to lower-performing agents.
- **Internalization:** Workers showed performance gains even when the tool was removed, suggesting human capital growth.
